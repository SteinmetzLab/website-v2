Ethics

Ethical considerations for fairness and justice in science

American academia has systematically excluded Black and Indigenous people of color. The fields of systems and computational neuroscience in particular exclude women. As a lab we are committed to making neuroscience a more open and inclusive field.

All lab members are encouraged to spend a portion of their work hours on mentoring, outreach and education, including participating in the lab's reading group on these topics.

Our action plan

  • Improving support for junior scientists from under-represented groups.
  • Working to improve departmental hiring policies.
  • Improving representation in our journal club, in the citations in our papers, and in the seminar series we attend.

In practice

We took part in a faculty search for our department, and wrote up the practices we used to reduce bias and broaden the pool: a detailed review of that search (PDF).

We host a high-school researcher every summer through the BRIGHT-UP program, and several of our undergraduates have gone on to fellowships and graduate programs — see where people went.
