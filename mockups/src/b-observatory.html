Neurobiology & Biophysics · Seattle

We watch the whole brain at once, and ask which parts actually decide.

Perception, cognition and action are not local. We record thousands of neurons across dozens of regions simultaneously, image the entire dorsal cortex, and switch circuits off to find out which ones matter.

Research

Four instruments, one question.

Brain-wide electrophysiology

Neuropixels probes record hundreds of neurons each, at single-spike resolution, from cortex down through midbrain. We help develop the probes themselves and the software that makes their data usable.

Neuropixels 1.0, 2.0, Ultra and Opto · Kilosort · the Neuropixels Consortium

Cortex-wide imaging

Calcium imaging across the whole dorsal surface, so that whatever we find in one recording site can be placed in the context of everything else that was happening.

SVD-compressed movies · hemodynamic correction · simultaneous optogenetics

Causal circuit dissection

Anatomy tells us what could carry a signal; optogenetics tells us what does. We trace single-neuron projections and then silence them during behavior.

Single-neuron reconstructions in the midbrain reticular formation

Behavior worth modelling

Mice trained on two-alternative visual discrimination, with enough trials and enough control that the psychophysics can be fit properly — and compared across labs.
