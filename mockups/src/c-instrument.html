Brain-wide circuits for perception, cognition and action

We record thousands of neurons across the whole mouse brain at once, image the entire dorsal cortex, and switch circuits off to find out which ones actually matter. And we build the instruments and software that make it possible.

Methods Four instruments, one question
Electrophysiology141 units

Brain-wide Neuropixels recording

Hundreds of neurons per probe at single-spike resolution, from cortex through midbrain. We help develop the probes and the sorting software the field runs on.

Imaging35 Hz

Cortex-wide calcium imaging

The whole dorsal surface at once, so a single recording site can be read in the context of everything else happening in cortex.

ManipulationOptogenetics

Causal circuit dissection

Trace single-neuron projections, then silence them during behavior to separate correlation from cause.

Behavior2-AFC

Psychophysics worth modeling

Two-alternative visual discrimination with enough trials and enough control that the behavior can be fit properly and compared across labs.
