Teaching

Courses and workshops

The lab contributes to the education of UW students and to training across the wider neuroscience community.

  • NEUSCI 490

    Every autumn, UW

    Seminar in Computational Neuroscience, an undergraduate course Nick teaches each fall.

  • UW–Allen Neuropixels Workshop

    Annual, three days

    Co-hosted by the lab and open to participants worldwide. We run the third day here, with advanced Neuropixels lectures, live experiment demos, and hands-on data processing tutorials. Workshop page.

  • UCL Neuropixels Course

    Annual, London

    Nick and other lab members contribute regularly to this international course. Course page, and recordings of recent years on YouTube.

  • Other UW courses

    Guest lectures

    NEURO 502 (Neurobiology), NEURO 527 (Current Topics in Neurobiology and Behavior) and CSE 528 (Computational Neuroscience).

  • International summer courses

    Faculty

    Transylvania Experimental Neuroscience Summer School (Cluj, Romania), Interacting with Neural Circuits (Cajal, Lisbon, Portugal), Paris Neuro Course (Paris, France), Linking Neural Circuits and Behavior (Cajal, Bordeaux, France), Neural Data Science (Cold Spring Harbor, NY, USA), and the Summer Workshop for the Dynamic Brain (Friday Harbor, WA, USA).
